Source guide

This source serves as an educational guide to the fundamental principles of **ecology and ecosystem dynamics**, categorizing the natural world into **biotic (living)** and **abiotic (non-living)** components. It provides a detailed taxonomy of species roles, ranging from **keystone species** that maintain environmental stability and **indicator species** that signal ecosystem health to **invasive species** that disrupt local balances. The text further explores the concept of a **niche**, defining it as the specific functional role or "work" an organism performs to survive and grow within its habitat. By tracing the hierarchical organization of life from **individual cells to the global biosphere**, the material illustrates how diverse communities interact with their physical surroundings to form a cohesive **ecological system**. Through these definitions, the source aims to provide students with a structured framework for understanding the **interconnectedness of life** and the necessity of conservation.
